LifeLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

LifeLink is a blood donation and donor-matching application intended to help connect people who need blood with potential blood donors in the Philippines. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use LifeLink.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, LifeLink may process:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and information used to authenticate your account.
  • Profile information: Middle name, blood type, profile picture, donor availability, and other details you choose to provide.
  • Blood requests: Blood type needed, descriptions, images, requested dates, and hospital or location details.
  • Messages: Chat messages and attachments exchanged through the application.
  • Hospital information: Hospital names, addresses, and location coordinates used by hospital-related features.
  • Technical information: Information required for authentication, security, troubleshooting, and app operation, where collected.

Please avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information in posts, messages, or uploaded images.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Manage user accounts.
  • Display donor profiles and blood donation requests.
  • Help users find potential blood donors.
  • Facilitate communication between users.
  • Show relevant hospital and location information.
  • Send relevant application notifications.
  • Maintain application functionality and help prevent misuse.

3. How Information Is Shared

Some information may be visible to other users to support LifeLink's features. For example, profile details and blood requests may be displayed to help users connect. Messages and attachments may be accessible to participants in the relevant conversation. Contact information may be shared through particular app features or interactions.

LifeLink may use service providers, including Supabase, for services such as authentication, database hosting, and file storage, depending on the app configuration. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users and the service.

LifeLink does not sell personal information as a business practice.

4. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored on third-party infrastructure used to operate LifeLink. Reasonable measures are intended to protect information, but no online transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain necessary records, address disputes, or meet legal obligations. You may request account deletion and deletion of associated personal information by contacting the developer using the contact details below. Some information may need to be retained where legally required.

Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete your account or server-stored information.

6. Children's Privacy

LifeLink is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. Please contact the developer if you believe a child has provided personal information.

7. Third-Party Services

LifeLink may rely on third-party services, such as Supabase or mapping services, to provide app features. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Users in the Philippines may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012, subject to its requirements and exceptions.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as LifeLink changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
